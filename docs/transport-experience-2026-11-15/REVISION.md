# Final flow and motion revision

This supersedes the earlier status-first bus page and expanding office register described in REPORT.md. The user rejected both compositions as confusing and requested a clear Bus → Live tracking flow, a smaller upper office panel, and purposeful driver animation. Follow this document for the final implementation.

Parents `/bus/` is now an overview: route/vehicle, own stop, scheduled morning pickup, a brief trip status, one Live tracking action, crew contacts and the timetable. It contains no map or expanded tracking feed. Live tracking opens `/bus/tracking/`, with a dedicated title/back link, child status, approximate arrival only for fresh valid data, a visible 220px map, last-update state and driver-reported delay. It does not repeat the entire crew and stops register. Missing data, failed connection, no active trip and demo remain explicit. Home retains the compact child-scoped bus link.

The office panel is a short Bus activity overview with at most two active routes, prioritizing those needing attention. The full route-management list stays visible below it. View status opens all trip statuses; selecting a route opens one bounded native dialog near the top (72px on phones, 80px on desktop). It contains only the route's trip, next stop, freshness, recorded progress and useful contact/location actions. Escape, focus containment and top-layer behavior are supplied by the native modal dialog. The view is shared by desktop ERP and staff ERP. No whole-page inline expansion remains.

Driver page transitions last 220ms with only a 5px rise; selection and press feedback last 140–200ms. A saved child mark has a short check-scale response. Dialog entry is subtle. These are action/navigation signals, not looping decorative effects. Reduced-motion preference disables these transitions.

Trip record keeps its date picker stable and remounts only the selected day's heading and records. Each day has a visible date and record count, followed by individual morning/afternoon run records with start/end times, state and recorded stops/children. The content transition lasts 180–200ms and cannot display another day's response under the selected date. An end time is called “Ended”, not proof that the bus reached the school or completed every drop.

Salary disclosure remains mounted and interpolates a grid row from 0fr to 1fr in 180ms ease-out, with matching arrow rotation. It has no spring/overshoot or arbitrary maximum-height timer. Buttons expose `aria-expanded` and associated content IDs; collapsed content is hidden from accessibility reading. Date changes and salary disclosure do not trigger network writes.

Map motion previously combined immediate marker jumps with independently eased camera movement. Both mobile maps now interpolate only between received fixes with requestAnimationFrame; cancellation begins the next transition at the currently displayed point. Nearby ordered road vertices can shape the interpolation; an implausible detour or a jump over600m is not animated across the map. Reduced motion applies the received point immediately. Heading uses the shortest angular change. No extrapolation beyond the latest fix occurs. Driver camera uses the same900ms timing, north-up35° pitch and a less aggressive zoom, avoiding constant rotation and high-pitch jitter. Demo advances about5.5m each700ms, rather than45m (the previous demo looked implausibly fast and discontinuous). Parent interpolation is1000ms between received snapshots; it cannot make sparse real telemetry continuous in the real world.

The motion utility has deterministic tests for road-path interpolation, final bounds, shortest heading, cancellation, frame progress and large-jump handling. Browser captures alone do not establish frame rate or actual driving performance; the focused driver harness also samples intermediate marker transforms and salary heights.

Research: [progressive disclosure](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/progressive-disclosure/) supports keeping secondary tracking/detail behind a deliberate action; [Material motion](https://m3.material.io/styles/motion) informed coherent transitions; [W3C reduced-motion technique](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/css/C39.html) informed preference handling. These are adaptations to the existing product, not a claim of formal usability validation.

Latest synthetic transport harness:25 captures, zero observed exceptions/overflow/missing contracts, no business writes. It verifies overview-to-tracking navigation, bounded upper office detail, all statuses, refresh error/recovery, teacher exclusion, child isolation and tracking recovery. Earlier screenshots in the folder are historical; use gallery.html's revised captures.

No push, deployment, migration, seed, live trip or real notification delivery was performed. AI assistant remains untouched. Actual outdoor GPS, background suspension and physical-device smoothness still need device verification.

## Soft swipe feedback refinement

Removed the breakdown/driver-reported sentence from office detail; the compact location link is now10px at the upper-right. Refined SlideConfirm to a60px pill track with a circular48px handle, direct GPU-translated pointer tracking, gently fading/slightly moving label and320ms exponential settling without overshoot. Full release latches for170ms before the action; partial/cancelled swipes return silently. A custom two-sine harmonic cue fades in/out over about0.2s at low gain, rather than a noise burst or stock alert. Context creation/resume occurs only inside an explicit gesture. The cue confirms the gesture; server persistence retains its own pending/error state. Unsupported or muted audio never blocks the action.
